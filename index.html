Mumbai Surfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Mumbai Surfer: Local Train Run ("the game") is made by Hash for Gamers Private Limited ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, transmit, store on our servers or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: chapters unlocked, coins, missions, high scores, selected runner and outfit, and your sound and graphics settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google's handling of data related to installing apps is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
