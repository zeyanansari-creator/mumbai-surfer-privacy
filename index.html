Mumbai Surfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Mumbai Surfer: Local Train Run ("the game") is made by Hash for Gamers Private Limited ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

What we collect

We do not run servers that store information about you, and the game has no user accounts or in-app purchases. Two Google services built into the game collect some data from your device:

  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (to revive after being caught, or for free coins). AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions, and crash and performance information, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Firebase Analytics records anonymous gameplay events (for example, a run started or ended, the station reached, a power-up collected) together with an app instance identifier and basic device information (model, operating system, app version, country). We use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. See Firebase's privacy information.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to identify you.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone the game does not ask to track you, so apps cannot use your advertising identifier. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, or stop all collection by uninstalling the game.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: chapters unlocked, coins, missions, high scores, selected runner and outfit, and your sound and graphics settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Third parties

Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. The game is distributed through the App Store and Google Play, whose own policies cover installing apps.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
